# Advanced Rizal activities — content approval guide

Seeded Case Files, Dispatches, Editorial Room, Dapitan Workshop, and chest artifacts ship as **`approvalStatus: draft`**. They intentionally use placeholders instead of invented historical quotations.

## What you must approve or provide

For each activity in Teacher studio:

1. Replace every `[DRAFT]`, `[Placeholder]`, and `[Teacher: …]` string.
2. Supply citations you stand behind (edition, archive, or clearly labeled classroom paraphrase).
3. Set `approvalStatus` to `approved` only after review.
4. Publish only after the module publish check no longer flags draft content.

### Case Files (#43)

- 3–5 source records with real excerpts you approve
- Accepted evidence combinations per defensible conclusion
- Sourced debrief text

### Dispatches from Europe (#44)

- Stop briefings and excerpts (no fabricated letters)
- Unlock order and learning objectives per stop
- Keep the map schematic; do not claim a surveyed itinerary without evidence

### Editorial Room (#45)

- Historical briefing that names modern assumptions students might import
- At least two accepted argument structures
- Specific feedback on weak/distractor pieces
- Do not score a preferred modern political opinion

### Dapitan Workshop (#46)

- Keep invented budgets labeled as game assumptions
- Approve the historical comparison debrief and source references
- Never present a numeric “best Rizal decision” as historical truth

### Artifact chests (#47)

- Artifact title, kind, summary, provenance, optional body/image
- Stable `artifact.id` (repeat completion must not duplicate rewards)
- Optional cosmetic `journalCoverId` (no loot boxes / paywalls)
